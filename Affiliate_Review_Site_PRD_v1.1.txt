1. Executive Summary
This PRD defines the requirements for building a modern, AI-powered affiliate review platform that generates recurring passive income through SEO-driven content. The platform mimics the effectiveness of a top-tier WordPress affiliate blog while leveraging a modern React/TypeScript/Supabase architecture for performance, scalability, and developer velocity.
The platform will publish expert reviews of AI tools and SaaS products, rank on Google for high-intent buyer keywords, capture email subscribers, and monetize through affiliate commissions, sponsored placements, and display advertising.
Vision
Become a recognized, SEO-authoritative affiliate media brand in the AI tools niche — generating $5,000+/month in recurring affiliate revenue within 18–24 months.

7. SEO Requirements
7.1 Technical SEO
Auto-generated sitemap.xml (updated on publish)
robots.txt with crawl rules
Canonical URLs on all pages
Open Graph + Twitter Card metadata on every page
Structured data: Article, Review, Product, FAQ, BreadcrumbList schemas
Core Web Vitals optimized: LCP < 2.5s, CLS < 0.1, FID < 100ms
7.2 Content SEO
Single H1 per page containing primary keyword
H2/H3 hierarchy for sub-topics and FAQ
Internal links: minimum 3 per article, automated suggestions in editor
Image alt text field required before publish
Estimated reading time displayed on posts
Last-updated date visible and in schema
